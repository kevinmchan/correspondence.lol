    How to play
    • The grid is split into coloured districts. One letter is hidden in every row, every column and every district.
    • Letters never touch each other, not even diagonally.
    • Players take turns, in any order: anyone may go next except the player who just moved. A turn is one action: add a cross where a letter can't be, remove a cross, or guess where a letter is.
    • A right guess stays on the board, and the rest of its row, column and district is crossed out for you. A wrong guess costs the team one of its three hearts.
    • Find every letter before the hearts run out and you all win.
    • In a room, everyone in the room can play, each on their own device, and the game waits between turns for as long as you like.
