Postmarks

A co-operative puzzle. Pass the device and take turns.

Players

    How to play
    • The grid is split into coloured districts. One letter is hidden in every row, every column and every district.
    • Letters never touch each other, not even diagonally.
    • Players take turns. A turn is one action: add a cross where a letter can't be, remove a cross, or guess where a letter is.
    • A right guess stays on the board. A wrong guess costs the team one of its three hearts.
    • Find every letter before the hearts run out and you all win.
